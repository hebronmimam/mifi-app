Speed on the Now screen

The screen as it is today, then three ways to show download and upload. The numbers move the way the real MiFi’s do: in bursts, averaged over the last 3 s. Pick a load to see each one quiet and busy.

Before

Today. Download is the big number, upload a small line under it; both are lines over the last 60 s. The ring is the battery.

A · Lit ticks

The dial’s own tick marks light up with the download, on a scale that gives slow speeds room: 1, 10 and 100 Mbps sit a third apart. Upload lights the inner ring. A dim tick holds the recent peak. The outer ring stays the battery.

B · Dot equaliser

The line chart becomes a dot matrix: one column a second, download rising in lime, upload falling in blue. A burst reads as a burst instead of a spike. The dial is as it is today.

C · Battery first

The dial holds the battery, number in the middle. Download and upload get a tile each with a segment bar on the same scale as A, and the last minute is two overlapping waves.
